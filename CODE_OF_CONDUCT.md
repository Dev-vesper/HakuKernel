# Code of Conduct

HakuKernel welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to
help keep them constructive and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, professional, and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique technical ideas and changes, not the people proposing them.
- Explain disagreements clearly and remain open to evidence and alternatives.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information without
  their permission.
- Follow the repository's contribution and security-reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate
intimidation, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private
information are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive or bad-faith behavior may
also be addressed by maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this policy, close
disruptive discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect
the project community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository
maintainers through GitHub. Do not use public issue reports for sensitive
personal information.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and handle them as discreetly as
the available project tools allow. This policy applies to project repositories,
issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces used for HakuKernel work.
